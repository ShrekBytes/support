Overview

The potato is one of Bangladesh's most widely cultivated and consumed vegetables, grown chiefly in Munshiganj, Bogura and Rangpur. A staple of everyday Bengali cooking, it appears in everything from bhorta and curries to snacks like singara and chop. Prices fluctuate seasonally with the November–March harvest and are tracked here across regions and shops.

About

BRAC University is a private university established in 2001 and affiliated with BRAC, one of the world's largest development organisations. Known for its Computer Science, Business and Architecture programs, it operates a permanent campus in Merul Badda, Dhaka, alongside liberal-arts-style general education requirements uncommon among private universities in the country.
